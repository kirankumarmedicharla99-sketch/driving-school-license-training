Everything You Need to Know.

Clear answers to common questions about learner permits, lesson scheduling, dual-control safety vehicles, and practical driving tests.

While national averages range between 35 and 45 hours, most DRIVEPRO students complete our structured 30-Hour Car License Training or 20-Hour Beginner Course combined with guided practice. During your first session, your instructor will evaluate your natural coordination and provide a transparent hour estimate tailored to your pace.

A manual driving license legally entitles you to drive both manual and automatic vehicles, making it a versatile choice. However, automatic license training removes clutch control and gear stalling completely, allowing nervous learners or urban commuters to reach test readiness significantly faster. We offer modern Euro NCAP 5-star vehicles in both transmissions.
